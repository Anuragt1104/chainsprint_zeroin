# ChainSprint · Product Vision

## Problem
Crypto coordination today is either hyper-financialized dashboards or noisy social feeds. Friend groups and creator-led communities lack a motivating space that translates onchain activity into shared progress, accountability, and emotional support. Without context, users churn, miss opportunities, or take unsustainable risks.

## Solution Overview
ChainSprint is a social companion for multi-chain wallets that turns Zerion's portfolio intelligence into collaborative "sprints." Crews track risk-adjusted goals, receive real-time nudges, and celebrate together. The experience mixes fitness-style streaks with transparent copy trading.

## Core Value Propositions
- **Motivational loops:** Squad challenges with risk-adjusted scoring keep both veterans and newcomers engaged.
- **Emotional storytelling:** Decoded transactions become short narratives so crews can respond with reactions, voice notes, or advice.
- **Transparent mirroring:** Real-time Zerion data powers optional "Shadow Moves" that explain the *why* behind trades before followers copy them.
- **Safety rails:** Contextual risk tags and budget caps discourage blind degen behavior and build trust.

## Target Personas
1. **Alpha Crews** – tight-knit friends chasing airdrops or yield. Need coordination, transparency, and supportive competition.
2. **Creator Squads** – KOLs/DAOs onboarding fans. Require structured programs, highlights, and monetizable exclusives.
3. **Learning Circles** – Newcomers exploring Solana + EVM. Seek guided discovery with low-friction insights.

## North Star Metrics
- Weekly active crews
- Sprint completion rate
- Retention of new wallets after 4 weeks
- Copy action conversion (shadow → executed)

## Proof of Adoption Strategy
- Start with invite-only Alpha Crews drawn from degen Telegram communities; offer analytics overlays that surface brag-worthy stats.
- Partner with mid-tier creators hosting “public sprints” where top moments surface on social media.
- Layer in unlockable badges tied to onchain achievements (powered by Zerion positions/PnL) to drive streak behavior.

## Why We Win Hackathons
- Demonstrates deep Zerion integration (PnL, positions, transactions, webhooks) with a unique social UX.
- Marries consumer emotion with reliable data infrastructure.
- Clear roadmap for monetization and scaling beyond the demo.
